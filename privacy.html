Privacy Policy

Effective: April 27, 2026. Last updated: April 27, 2026.

This Privacy Policy describes how the Motorsport Vault content automation pipeline ("we", "our", or "the system") handles information in connection with publishing video content to third-party platforms including but not limited to YouTube and TikTok, on behalf of a single creator account operated by Ruehl Media.

1. Who we are

Motorsport Vault is a personal content channel publishing short-form motorsport history videos. The publishing pipeline is operated for a single creator account (@MotorsportVault on YouTube and TikTok). The contact for any privacy questions is ruehlmedia.ops@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

The pipeline does not collect, store, or process data from end users or video viewers. The only personal data involved is the data the operator (the channel owner) provides to authenticate with third-party platforms — specifically OAuth tokens issued by YouTube and TikTok, stored locally on the operator's machine. These tokens authorize the pipeline to upload videos to the operator's own accounts and nothing else.

3. Source content

Video content is composed from:

  • Stock footage licensed from Pexels under their commercial-use license.
  • Synthetic narration generated by ElevenLabs from scripts written by the operator.
  • Auto-generated captions transcribed locally using OpenAI Whisper, then burned into the video.

No user-generated content, no third-party copyrighted material, and no content from other creators is republished without license.

4. Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or license any data to third parties. The pipeline transmits video files only to the platforms the operator has explicitly authorized (currently YouTube and TikTok), and only to the operator's own accounts on those platforms.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking pixels.

6. Children's privacy

The pipeline produces educational/historical motorsport content marked as not made for children where required by platform policy (YouTube COPPA flag set to false). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

7. Your rights

If you believe content published by Motorsport Vault includes material you own or otherwise affects your rights, contact ruehlmedia.ops@gmail.com and we will respond within 7 days.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when underlying platform requirements or processing details change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Email: ruehlmedia.ops@gmail.com
